You weren't always a crow. Roll 2d6 (first die = left column, second = right) or pick one.

2 Characteristics

Your background sets one characteristic to 2. The other two are either a 1 and a 0, or a -1 and a 2.

4 Expertises & Stamina

Spend a use after a roll to improve the result by one tier. Uses return when you finish a rest.

5 Traits

Browse trait trees

Starting traits (top row) cost 500 XP. Other traits must connect by a line to a trait you already own in the same tree. New crows start with 0 XP; set Total XP in the Advancement section if your Ref lets a new crow start with more.

6 Equipment & Inventory

Every crow starts with an empty coin purse, a knife, a rope, six rations, and 3d6 gc, plus their background's gear. Click a card to select it, then click a slot to move it (or drag it). Hands can't hold stacks; multi-slot items need adjacent slots of one type.

Not carried (drop here to set aside; left behind at home)

7 Village & Connection

Make an NPC in your village who matters to your crow, and pick the benefit they give you.

8 Advancement (optional)

New crows start at 0 TXP. If every other crow has 5,000+ TXP, the Ref can start a new crow at the lowest TXP in the party. Enter Total XP here to apply bonuses and buy traits.
